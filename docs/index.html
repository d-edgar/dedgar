One place to compare AI models and work through an idea. Connect cloud providers or local models, then use a structured brainstorm to get from options to a decision.

macOS 14+ · Bring your own API keys or local models

  1. Compare answers

    Send one prompt to 2–4 models. See their responses, speed, token usage, and estimated costs side by side.

  2. Work through disagreements

    Assign roles, shortlist ideas, and ask participants to identify assumptions and propose small tests.

  3. Keep the decision

    Save a decision brief, add your reasoning, and return later to record what happened. Export to Markdown or PDF.

About

IT Leader & Mac Admin

David has been working in IT since 2006, when he began his degree at Old Dominion University. A computer hobbyist and technology enthusiast for as long as he can remember, he turned that passion into a career spanning nearly two decades across endpoint management, automation, and infrastructure.

Currently the Manager of Technical Support Services at Christopher Newport University (CNU), he's spent the past seven plus years leading and growing teams, combining hands on technical skills with people focused leadership. He specializes in Apple device management with Jamf Pro and loves building tools that make life easier for IT teams.

Outside of work, David is an avid runner and enjoys sharing knowledge with the Mac admin community at conferences like ACCS.
